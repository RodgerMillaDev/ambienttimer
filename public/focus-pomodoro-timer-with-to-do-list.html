How does our focus pomodoro timer work?

We designed our focus timer based on the well-known Pomodoro Technique by Francesco Cirillo. This technique involves focusing on a single task for 25 minutes. When your timer rings to signal the end of the session, you take a short a 5-minute break.

After repeating these steps four times, you can take a longer break for 15 to 30 minutes.

Is the Focus Pomodoro Timer for Everyone?

To be honest, it’s not. A focus pomodoro timer might not be a good option if you’re working in a fast-paced environment or collaborating with other people. It might not also work well when you work on projects with many sudden changes.

Additionally, the Pomodoro technique might have a slight learning curve for some people. You might need some time to adjust and integrate it into your workflow or study pattern.

But after a brief adjustment period, you can greatly benefit from the technique relatively quickly. All in all, it’s a great choice for most people. In fact, Francesco Cirillo claims that his technique has helped more than two million people worldwide achieve greater focus and productivity.
